Using these figures responsibly

SUTA rates depend on each employer’s experience and state surcharges. Workers’ comp depends on class codes, carrier and loss history; an index is not a rate anyone can buy. State × industry wages and state health premiums will be added only with a traceable source and year — until then they are shown as not published rather than estimated.
