What to do, and by when

  • Nov 15, 2026

    Deadline to file a Declaration of Intent if you plan to use a private plan instead of the state plan (per Jackson Lewis). Private plans may not withhold more from employees than the state rate.

  • Before your first 2027 payroll

    Register in the FAMLI portal if you have at least one Maryland employee. A payroll provider or CPA cannot register for you, but can file for you afterward under a power of attorney.

  • One pay period before deductions

    If you will withhold the employee share, give employees written notice at least one pay period before deductions begin.

  • Jan 1, 2027

    Contributions begin on wages paid from this date: 0.45% employer share at 15 or more employees, up to 0.45% withheld from pay, both capped at the $192,300 Social Security wage base.

  • Apr 30, 2027

    First quarterly payment and wage and hour report due, covering wages paid January 1 to March 31, 2027. Reports and payments are filed electronically every quarter after that.

  • July 2027

    All employers give employees notice of FAMLI rights, six months before benefits begin; then at hire and once a year.

  • Jan 3, 2028

    Benefits become available, no later than this date.

Questions employers ask

Who pays Maryland FAMLI?

The total contribution is 0.90% of wages up to the Social Security wage base ($192,300 in 2027). Employers with 15 or more employees pay a 0.45% employer share and may withhold up to the 0.45% employee share from pay. Employers may also pay all or part of the employee share themselves; the state notes this can have tax consequences, so check with your accountant.

Do employers with fewer than 15 employees pay FAMLI?

They owe no employer share, but they still remit the 0.45% employee share for every employee in a position localized in Maryland. Even an employer with one Maryland employee must register.

How are the 15 employees counted?

Count employees inside and outside Maryland; independent contractors do not count. For 2027, size is measured each quarter from the Quarterly Wage and Hour Report. From 2028 it is the average of the previous four quarters, and an average below 15 sets the lower rate for the following year.

What is the FAMLI wage cap for 2027?

Contributions apply to wages up to the Social Security wage base, which is $192,300 for 2027. At or above the cap the employer share is $865.35 per employee per year.

When is the first FAMLI payment due?

Contributions and wage and hour reports are filed every quarter. The first payment, for wages paid January 1 to March 31, 2027, is due April 30, 2027.

Can employees opt out of FAMLI?

No. Participation is mandatory for employees working in positions localized in Maryland.

Can my payroll provider or CPA file FAMLI for me?

A third-party agent such as a CPA or payroll, HR or benefits provider cannot register on your behalf. After you register, you can authorize one through a power of attorney to file quarterly reports and contributions.

How the calculator works

  • Each employee’s wage is capped at the 2027 Social Security wage base of $192,300, then multiplied by the 0.45% employer rate (15 or more employees only) and the 0.45% employee rate.
  • The quarterly payment is the annual total divided by four, which assumes pay is spread evenly across the year. Bonuses or seasonal staff shift money between quarters.
  • Headcount uses the number you enter. In 2027 the state measures size each quarter, so an employer near 15 can cross the line mid-year.
  • Not modeled: private plans, mid-year hires and departures, and rates after 2027. The rate is capped at 1.2% by law; Maryland Labor announces each year’s rate starting November 2027.
  • Rates, cap and dates are read from dmv-index-2026.json, the same file behind the DMV Employer Cost Index.
