Monopolistic workers’ comp: employers in Wyoming buy coverage from the state fund, so a PEO master policy cannot replace it.

SUTA note: New employers pay their industry's average rate (DOL: IndAvg), so no single 2026 rate exists. The default is the 2025 reference value — replace it with the rate on your notice.

The workers’ comp index prices one fixed basket of job classes so states can be compared; your rate depends on your class codes, carrier and loss history. Office-heavy firms run well below the index; construction and transportation run well above it.
