Privacy · draft · October 27, 2026

Useful comparisons, without a sales funnel.

Draft notice. This describes how the site works today. Retention periods and the request process below are proposed and will be confirmed before emailed reports launch.

Using the tools

The Cost Lab and Quote Comparator work without an account or an email. Their arithmetic runs in your browser; the inputs you type are not sent to us unless you request an emailed report.

If you use the business-email prefill

We record the email you submit, its domain, the time, and the public company details we found (name, state, industry, approximate headcount). Only the domain — never the full address — is used for research. Personal email domains are refused. You can correct every prefilled field.

If you request an emailed report

We use your business email and the assumptions you submitted to build a PDF and Excel file, and a transactional email provider to send them. We record the request and whether the provider accepted it. The report is a planning estimate, not a quote.

A possible survey invitation

After a report request we may send one invitation to a short survey about 30 days later, with a way to opt out. Using the calculator or the prefill does not enroll you in any marketing sequence, and none is running today.

Who processes requests

Cloudflare (hosting, security, abuse prevention via Turnstile, and Cloudflare Web Analytics page-view measurement), Anthropic (company-domain research), Google (the private spreadsheet that records lookups and report requests), and the transactional email provider used for reports. We do not sell or forward this information to PEO companies, and using the tools does not trigger a sales call.

Retention and your requests Proposed

Proposed: usage and report-request records kept up to 12 months, opt-out records kept as long as needed to honor them. To ask for access, correction, suppression or deletion, use the contact page with “privacy request” in the subject.
